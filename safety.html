Guidelines
Trust is part of the product

Safer mentorship starts before the first session.

MentEducation is designed with mentor review, account safeguards, platform payment records, reporting tools and Owner oversight so users have clearer ways to understand who they are working with and what to do when something goes wrong.

Our safety framework

✓

Mentor review

Mentor applications can be reviewed for identity, professional information, credentials and other platform requirements before marketplace approval.

⌁

Platform payments

Keep paid MentEducation sessions within the platform so bookings, credits, payouts, cancellations and disputes have an auditable record.

!

Reporting & review

Users should be able to report concerning behavior or session issues for Owner review, with administrative controls available when intervention is necessary.

◉

Account controls

Authentication, password recovery and role-based access help protect mentee, mentor and administrative experiences.

★

Reviews & accountability

Post-session feedback helps the marketplace build useful quality signals while moderation protects against misuse.

↺

Fair booking policies

Cancellation, rescheduling, no-show, credit and compensation rules aim to respect both a mentee's circumstances and a mentor's reserved time.

Keep communication and payment on-platform.

Platform records make it easier to investigate booking, payment and conduct concerns. Users should never feel pressured to bypass MentEducation protections for a paid MentEducation session.

When to report something

Conduct concerns

Harassment, threats, discrimination, inappropriate sexual behavior, exploitation, intimidation or other behavior that violates platform standards.

Misrepresentation

False credentials, impersonation, misleading professional claims or material information that appears intentionally deceptive.

Payment or booking issues

Disputed attendance, improper off-platform payment requests, suspected payment misuse or unresolved session-status problems.

Immediate danger

MentEducation's reporting process is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services.
