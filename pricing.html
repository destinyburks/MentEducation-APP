How the mentor fee works

Mentors choose their own session prices. MentEducation charges a 15% platform fee on completed paid sessions. The mentor's dashboard will show earnings and payout status clearly.

Credits, cancellations & no-shows

MentEducation uses account credits in certain cancellation and repeated no-show situations so eligible value can remain available for future mentorship while respecting mentors' reserved time. Exact outcomes depend on the booking policy and circumstances, with Owner override available for approved exceptions.

Before a mentee submits payment, checkout should display the mentor's session price, applied credits, amount due today and the applicable cancellation/no-show policy.
