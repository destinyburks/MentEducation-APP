How MentEducation Works

Real guidance, built around your goals.

MentEducation brings mentees and experienced mentors together through a structured marketplace designed to make discovering, booking and delivering mentorship simple.

For MenteesFor Mentors

For mentees

Find the right mentor, choose the support you need and manage the entire mentorship experience from one account.

1

Create your account

Sign up for a free mentee account so your bookings, messages, credits and session history stay connected.

2

Find your mentor

Browse mentor profiles by expertise and goals. Review their background, services, pricing, availability and reviews.

3

Choose a session

Select a mentor-created service and choose an available date and time that works for you.

4

Book securely

Review the booking terms, confirm your appointment and complete secure checkout. Your selected time is temporarily held while you pay.

5

Meet & learn

Attend your scheduled mentorship session and receive focused, real-world guidance from your mentor.

6

Keep growing

Manage future sessions from your dashboard, communicate through MentEducation and leave a review after an eligible completed experience.

For mentors

Mentors move through an approval and onboarding process before becoming publicly bookable on MentEducation.

1

Create a mentor account

Choose “Become a Mentor,” confirm your email and begin an application connected to your MentEducation account.

2

Submit your application

Tell us about your professional background, expertise, credentials and approach to mentorship. Save and return whenever needed.

3

MentEducation review

Your application is reviewed before mentor tools and onboarding requirements are unlocked.

4

Complete onboarding

Finish your mentor profile, add services and pricing, set availability and complete required verification steps.

5

Publish your profile

After all required onboarding items are complete, your mentor profile can be published and made available for booking.

6

Mentor & earn

Receive bookings, manage sessions and messages, support mentees and track eligible earnings and payouts from your mentor dashboard.
