Privacy

Your information should have a clear purpose.

This page summarizes the privacy principles being built into MentEducation. A final launch-ready privacy policy should be reviewed for the platform's actual data practices and applicable legal requirements before public launch.

Information users provide

Account details, profile information, mentor application information, booking details, communications submitted to support, reviews and other information users choose to provide.

Platform activity

MentEducation may process information needed to operate authentication, bookings, payments, credits, payouts, safety review and platform performance.

Payments

Payment processing is intended to use integrated payment providers. MentEducation should avoid storing full payment-card details when the payment provider can securely process them.

Safety & administration

Relevant account, booking and transaction records may be reviewed when necessary to resolve disputes, investigate reports, prevent misuse or administer platform policies.

Data choices

Users should have clear ways to update account information and request appropriate account or privacy support.

Security

Role-based access, authentication controls and secure third-party infrastructure are part of the platform's security approach, but no online system can promise absolute security.

Pre-launch notice: This is a product-facing draft, not a substitute for final legal review. Before MentEducation's public launch, the policy should be finalized to match actual vendors, retention practices, state requirements, age eligibility and user rights.
