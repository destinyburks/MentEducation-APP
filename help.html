Frequently asked questions

How do I book a mentor?

Browse the mentor directory, open a mentor profile, choose a service, select an available date on the calendar, choose a time, review the booking details and complete checkout.

Can I use MentEducation credits toward a session?

Eligible wallet credits can be applied during checkout. The booking review shows the credit applied and remaining amount due before payment.

What happens if I need to reschedule?

Eligible bookings can follow the platform rescheduling flow. The exact options depend on timing, prior attendance history and the booking policy shown for the session.

What happens after a no-show?

MentEducation applies its no-show and rebooking policy based on the booking history. Repeated missed sessions can affect refund eligibility and may convert eligible value to account credits while compensating the mentor for reserved time.

How do mentors get paid?

Mentor earnings are tracked after eligible completed sessions and paid through the connected payout system. Mentors can view pending and paid amounts from Earnings.

How do I report a problem?

Use the platform reporting/support process or contact MentEducation. Booking and payment records can be reviewed by the Owner when intervention is required.

Still need help?

Contact MentEducation support with your account email and a short description of the issue. Do not send your password or full payment-card information.
