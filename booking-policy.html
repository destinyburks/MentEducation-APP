Booking & Cancellation Policy

These rules are designed to protect mentees' time, mentors' availability, and a fair booking experience across MentEducation.

Booking a session

When you select an available appointment and continue to checkout, MentEducation temporarily holds that appointment for 10 minutes. Your booking is not fully confirmed until the required checkout process is successfully completed.

Important: If checkout is not completed before the hold expires, the appointment may be released and become available for another mentee to book.

Before you confirm

Please review the mentor, service, date, time, duration, price, and any session-specific details before completing payment. By confirming a booking, you agree to the booking rules shown during checkout and this policy.

Cancellations

Mentees should cancel as early as possible when they cannot attend. The refund or account-credit treatment of a cancellation may depend on how close the cancellation occurs to the scheduled session and any terms displayed at booking. Where MentEducation provides a refund, credit, or other remedy, the applicable amount and method will be shown or communicated to the user.

Rescheduling

Eligible bookings may be rescheduled to another time offered by the mentor. Rescheduling is subject to mentor availability and any applicable cutoff or limits shown for the booking. A requested time is not guaranteed until the rescheduled appointment is confirmed.

Mentor cancellations

If a mentor cancels a confirmed session, the affected mentee will be given the applicable rebooking, account-credit, or refund options available for that booking. MentEducation may also help the mentee locate another mentor when appropriate.

No-shows and late arrivals

Mentees and mentors are expected to arrive on time and be prepared for the scheduled session. A participant who does not attend, or who arrives too late for the session to reasonably continue, may be treated as a no-show under the rules applicable to that booking. A late arrival does not automatically extend the scheduled end time.

Session interruptions and re-entry

If a participant is unexpectedly disconnected from a live session, they should attempt to rejoin promptly. MentEducation may provide a short re-entry period when supported by the session experience. Technical problems should be reported as soon as possible so they can be reviewed fairly.

Session completion

A successful payment does not by itself determine whether a session was properly completed. MentEducation may use booking records, attendance information, session status, user reports, and other relevant platform information to resolve completion questions or disputes.

Reviews

Reviews should reflect a genuine completed MentEducation experience. MentEducation may restrict, remove, or decline reviews that are fraudulent, abusive, unrelated to the session, or otherwise violate platform rules.

Disputes and support

If something goes wrong with a booking, users should contact MentEducation support promptly and provide relevant details. MentEducation may review booking records and information from both participants before determining the appropriate platform remedy.

Policy updates

MentEducation may update this policy as the platform develops. Material changes will apply according to the version in effect for the relevant booking or as otherwise communicated to users.
