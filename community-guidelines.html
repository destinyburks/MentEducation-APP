Community Guidelines

Respect the person behind every profile.

MentEducation exists to support productive, professional mentorship. These standards apply to mentees, mentors and other participants using the platform.

Be respectful

No harassment, threats, hate, discrimination, intimidation or unwanted sexual behavior. Disagreement is allowed; abuse is not.

Be truthful

Represent your identity, experience, credentials, services and intentions accurately. Do not impersonate others or intentionally mislead users.

Respect time

Attend scheduled sessions, communicate promptly when plans change and use the platform's rescheduling and cancellation tools.

Protect privacy

Do not share another user's private information, recordings or session details without appropriate permission.

Keep paid sessions on-platform

Do not pressure users to bypass MentEducation payment or booking protections for services discovered and booked through MentEducation.

Use mentorship responsibly

Mentors should stay within their competence and avoid presenting mentorship as licensed legal, medical, financial or other regulated professional advice when they are not qualified to provide it.

Violations can affect platform access.

MentEducation may review reports and booking records and may warn, restrict, suspend or remove accounts when necessary to protect the community. Owner review and emergency exceptions can be handled case by case.

See something concerning?

Report it instead of escalating the situation yourself. Include the relevant session or account information and a factual description of what occurred.
