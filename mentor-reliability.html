How escalation works

1st confirmed no-show: warning recorded.

2nd confirmed no-show: new bookings are temporarily restricted and the account is sent for Owner review.

3rd confirmed no-show: the mentor is suspended from accepting new bookings until Owner review.

Properly submitted advance cancellations and approved reschedules are handled separately and do not automatically count as no-shows.
